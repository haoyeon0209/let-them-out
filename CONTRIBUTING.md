# How to Contribute

**Let Them Out** welcomes every cultural ambassador.

## Quick Start
1. Find a text you want to translate or recreate. Any language, any era, any form.
2. Create your version. No standard answer required.
3. Sign your name and write one sentence about your cultural background.
4. Submit via:
   - **GitHub**: Fork this repo, add your folder under `submissions/`, and send a Pull Request.
   - **No GitHub?**: Email [your email] or DM me on [social media]. I will upload it for you within a week, with your name.

## Submission Format
Use the template at `templates/submission.md`.

## Quality
- Your name is your guarantee.
- AI comparison is optional but encouraged.
- Community feedback helps good work surface.

## Code of Conduct
Be respectful. We celebrate cultural diversity.
